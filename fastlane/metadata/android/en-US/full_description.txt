AirPlay TV turns your Android TV into an AirPlay receiver made for one thing: mirroring your iPhone to the TV to watch movies on the big screen.

- Screen mirroring from iPhone and iPad, with the phone's black bars cropped away so the movie fills the TV
- Smooth playback: steady motion with the sound kept in lip-sync
- A remote that can't stop the movie by accident: stopping from the TV asks for confirmation
- Audio-only mode for AirPlay music, with track info, cover art and playback controls
- Optional PIN, a prompt the first time a new device connects, and a list of allowed devices
- While one device is mirroring, others are told the TV is busy; the same device can reconnect straight away
- Settings over ADB

AirPlay Video (handing the TV a stream URL) is intentionally not supported: iOS keeps mirroring instead, which works with every video app. DRM-protected content (e.g. the Apple TV app) can't be mirrored.

Based on android-airplay-server and UxPlay. Not affiliated with Apple Inc.
